Built for Procurement Integrity

Every component designed to support officers without replacing their judgment.

🤖
AI Document Extraction
LLM-powered or offline FakeExtractor reads bid documents, extracts structured fields with bounding-box coordinates for evidence tracing.
⚙️
Deterministic Rule Engine
Plain Python rules — no hallucinations. Evaluates TURNOVER_MIN, CERT_VALIDITY, OEM_AUTH, MSME_EXEMPTION with full auditability.
🔐
Tamper-Evident Audit Trail
SHA-256 hash chain links every action. A single tampered row breaks the chain — detectable instantly via the /audit/verify endpoint.
✍️
Officer Override System
Officers can override any AI recommendation with mandatory written justification. All overrides are permanently logged and immutable.
🏛️
Mock Government Registries
Synthetic GSTN, MCA/Income Tax, and Udyam MSME registry cross-checks with realistic dummy responses for demo and testing.
📊
Compliance Scorecard
Weighted scoring across all criteria. Risk levels (LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH) computed from rule outcomes. MSME exemptions applied automatically.
01
AI Reads, Engine Decides

02
Officer Always Approves
System is DECISION SUPPORT ONLY. Never automatically disqualifies. Final legal authority rests with the procurement officer.
03
Offline-First
Works out-of-the-box with FakeExtractor reading sidecar JSONs. No internet required. Switch to LLM mode when needed.
04
Cryptographic Integrity
Every action appended to a SHA-256 hash-chained log. Tampering is detected immediately and alerts the verification endpoint.
